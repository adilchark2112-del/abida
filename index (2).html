ABIDA Analytics for Etsy Sellers
Personal Use Only

Your Etsy Stats
In Real Time

ABIDA is a personal Chrome Extension that gives Etsy shop owners instant access to their sales analytics directly from the browser.

⏱️

Live Sale Timer

Tracks exact time elapsed since your last sale, updated every second.

📊

Sales Breakdown

View sales count for the current hour, today, last 24 hours, and this week.

💰

Revenue Tracking

Daily and weekly revenue totals displayed clearly in your dashboard.

🏆

Top Products

Ranks your listings by sales performance to show what's working best.

👁️

View Counts

Displays which of your active listings are getting the most views.

🔄

Auto Refresh

Data updates automatically every 5 minutes using the Etsy API.

🔒 Privacy First

ABIDA only reads data from the authenticated shop owner's account using OAuth 2.0. It does not collect, share, or transmit any data to external servers. All information is stored locally on the user's device. The app never writes or modifies any Etsy data.
